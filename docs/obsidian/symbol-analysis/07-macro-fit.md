# 07 — Macro Fit

> Macro fit asks: does the current macro environment favor or disfavor this type of asset? An individually attractive stock in the wrong macro regime can still produce poor returns. A mediocre stock in the right regime can outperform.

## Current regime questions
- Where are we in the business cycle (early, mid, late, recession)?
- What is the Fed doing (cutting, holding, raising)?
- Is inflation accelerating, stable, or decelerating?
- Is growth accelerating, stable, or decelerating?
- Is credit tightening or loosening?
- Are real rates positive or negative?

## What macro favors what

| Macro environment | Favors | Disfavors |
|-------------------|--------|-----------|
| Low rates, growth accelerating | Growth tech, small caps, EM | Defensives, bonds, dollar |
| Rising rates, growth solid | Financials, cyclicals, value | Long-duration tech, REITs |
| Stagflation (high inflation + slow growth) | Energy, commodities, gold, defensives | Growth tech, long bonds, consumer discretionary |
| Recession + Fed cutting | Quality, dividend payers, long bonds | Cyclicals, leveraged companies |
| Strong dollar | US large caps, US-focused businesses | EM, multinationals, commodities |
| Risk-off / flight to safety | Treasuries, gold, USD, defensives | Tech, high-yield, EM |

## Sector sensitivity to macro factors

- **Technology:** sensitive to interest rates (long-duration cash flows) and growth expectations
- **Financials:** sensitive to yield curve shape and credit conditions
- **Energy:** sensitive to oil prices and global demand
- **Utilities:** sensitive to interest rates (bond proxies) and regulatory environment
- **Consumer Discretionary:** sensitive to consumer confidence and disposable income
- **Healthcare:** relatively defensive, less macro-sensitive
- **Consumer Staples:** defensive, low macro sensitivity
- **REITs:** sensitive to interest rates and real estate cycles

## Where to find current macro context

- **FRED (St. Louis Fed)** — all the key macro time series, free
- **Yardeni Research** — free weekly summaries of sector earnings and macro themes
- **FactSet Earnings Insight** — sector-level earnings revisions, free weekly PDF
- **BLS (Bureau of Labor Statistics)** — CPI, employment data monthly
- **BEA (Bureau of Economic Analysis)** — GDP, personal income quarterly
- **Your SignalForge regime classifier output** — automated macro read ([[../04 - Regime Classifier (phase1_v3)]])

## The macro fit question

> **The honest test:** Does the current macro environment support holding this asset for my expected holding period?
>
> **🟢 If yes:** macro is a tailwind. Reasonable position size justified.
>
> **🟡 If no:** macro is a headwind. Either skip the position, size smaller, or shorten holding period.
>
> **❓ If uncertain:** the analysis isn't complete. Don't force a decision from incomplete information.
