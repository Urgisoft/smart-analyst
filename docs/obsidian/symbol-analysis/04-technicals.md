# 04 — Technicals

> Technicals tell you what the price action is doing right now. They don't predict the future, but they tell you what other market participants are doing. Useful for entry/exit timing, not for whether to own something.

## Price location
- Current price vs 52-week range — near high, mid-range, or near low?
- Distance from 52-week high (in %)
- Distance from 52-week low (in %)
- All-time high date and price (context for current level)

## Trend indicators
- 50-day moving average — short-term trend reference
- 200-day moving average — long-term trend reference
- Position relative to MAs: above both = strong uptrend, below both = downtrend, mixed = consolidation
- Whether 50-day is above or below 200-day ("golden cross" / "death cross" relationships)

## Momentum indicators
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index):** 0-100 scale. Above 70 = overbought, below 30 = oversold
- **MACD:** trend + momentum combined
- **Rate of change** over 1-month, 3-month, 6-month, 12-month periods

## Aggregator views

Technical analysis aggregators combine many indicators into a single read. Useful for sanity-check but don't over-rely. Two good free sources:

- **Investing.com Technical Analysis** — Strong Sell to Strong Buy across 5 timeframes (5min, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly)
- **TradingView technical summary** — similar consolidation

> Note: Maximum bullish technical convergence is itself a contrarian warning, not just confirmation. When every indicator across every timeframe shows Strong Buy, you're at sentiment extreme territory.

## Volume context
- Today's volume vs average daily volume (1.0 = average, 2.0 = double normal)
- Volume trend over recent weeks (rising = increasing interest)
- Volume on up days vs down days (more on up days = accumulation)

## How to interpret

> **🟢 Bullish setup:** price above both moving averages, RSI 50-70 (not yet overbought), 50-day above 200-day, rising volume on up days, uptrend across multiple timeframes.

> **🔴 Bearish setup:** price below both moving averages, RSI below 50 and falling, 50-day below 200-day, rising volume on down days, downtrend across multiple timeframes.

> **🟡 Caution signal:** price at 52-week high + RSI above 70 + maximum bullish across all timeframes. This is sentiment extreme territory. Continuation possible; reversal probability rises.

## Where to find this data

- Fidelity Active Trader Pro — full technical suite
- TradingView — best free charting (basic tier)
- Investing.com — technical analysis summary
- StockCharts.com — pattern recognition, free for basics
