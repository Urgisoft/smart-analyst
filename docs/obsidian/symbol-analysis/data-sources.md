# Data Sources Reference

> Every check in the playbook has a source. Free sources cover almost everything; paid only matters at the edges. Bookmark these for easy access.

## Primary research platforms

| Source | Best for | Cost |
|--------|----------|------|
| Fidelity research | Most metrics in one place, options data, technical analysis | Free with Fidelity account |
| Morningstar | Fund portfolio analysis, style measures, fundamentals | Free for basics, paid premium |
| Yahoo Finance | Stock fundamentals, earnings dates, holdings | Free |
| TradingView | Charting and technical analysis | Free tier sufficient for most |
| Investing.com | Multi-timeframe technical aggregation | Free |

## Fundamentals & valuation

- **SEC EDGAR** — 10-K, 10-Q, proxy statements (authoritative source)
- **Company investor relations pages** — earnings calls, presentations
- **FactSet Earnings Insight** — weekly free PDF on S&P 500 sector earnings
- **Stratosphere.io / StockAnalysis.com** — clean fundamental data

## Technicals

- **TradingView** — free charting with all major indicators
- **StockCharts.com** — pattern recognition tools
- **Fidelity Active Trader Pro** — full technical suite if you're on Fidelity

## Sentiment & options

- **Fidelity options chains and analytics**
- **Barchart Unusual Options Activity** — free tier
- **MarketChameleon** — options flow
- **CBOE Volatility Indices page** — VIX, VVIX, term structure

## Institutional positioning

- **SEC EDGAR** — Form 4 (insiders), 13D/13G (5% holders), 13F (institutions), N-PORT (mutual funds), N-CSR (shareholder reports)
- **OpenInsider.com** — best free Form 4 aggregator with cluster buying
- **Whalewisdom.com** — best free 13F aggregator
- **Finviz Insider Trading** — alternative Form 4 view

## Macro context

- **FRED (St. Louis Fed)** — all macro time series, free
- **BLS** — CPI, employment data
- **BEA** — GDP, personal income
- **Yardeni Research** — free weekly research
- **CFTC.gov** — Commitments of Traders reports
- **FINRA** — short interest (biweekly), margin debt (monthly)
