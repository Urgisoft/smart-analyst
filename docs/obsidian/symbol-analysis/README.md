# Symbol Analysis Playbook

> Repeatable methodology for analyzing any stock, ETF, or fund. Six dimensions, decision framework, worksheet template.

## How to use this folder

**For a quick screen (15 min):** Open [[quick-screen]]. Seven fast checks. If a symbol fails the screen, skip it without doing the full work.

**For a full analysis:** Duplicate [[worksheet-template]], rename it `[TICKER]-[YYYY-MM-DD].md` (e.g., `NVDA-2026-05-17.md`), and fill in each section. The methodology pages below explain what each check means.

**For methodology reference:** Read sections [[01-what-is-it]] through [[08-decision-framework]] in order to understand the framework. Come back to specific sections when you need a refresher.

## The framework

Every symbol gets evaluated on six dimensions:

| # | Dimension | Question | Doc |
|---|-----------|----------|-----|
| 1 | What is it | Do I understand what I'm buying? | [[01-what-is-it]] |
| 2 | Fundamentals | Is the underlying quality strong? | [[02-fundamentals]] |
| 3 | Valuation | Am I paying a reasonable price? | [[03-valuation]] |
| 4 | Technicals | What is price action telling me? | [[04-technicals]] |
| 5 | Sentiment | Is positioning extreme? | [[05-sentiment-options]] |
| 6 | Structure | Are concentration and costs acceptable? | [[06-structure]] |
| 7 | Macro fit | Does environment favor this? | [[07-macro-fit]] |

Then combine in [[08-decision-framework]] for the position decision.

## The principle underneath

No single dimension decides the position. The combination tells you whether risk/reward is favorable. Most retail traders check 1-2 dimensions and miss the rest — the discipline is being systematic.

## Three principles to remember

**1. The discipline is being systematic, not being smart.** Most retail traders check fundamentals OR technicals OR sentiment — rarely all six dimensions. The systematic approach is the edge.

**2. Skip more than you trade.** If every symbol you analyze looks like a buy, you're not being selective. Most analysis should result in a Skip decision.

**3. The worksheet is the work.** Writing down each finding catches contradictions and missing data that mental analysis misses. Filling in the worksheet forces you to confront what you don't actually know.
