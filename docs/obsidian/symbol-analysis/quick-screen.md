# Quick Screen — 15-Minute Symbol Check

> When you don't have time for the full [[worksheet-template]] — a fast screen to decide whether deeper analysis is warranted. If a symbol fails this screen, skip it; the full analysis won't change the answer. If it passes, do the full work.

## The 7 quick checks

### 1. What is it (1 min)
- Is this a security type I understand?
- Is this even an appropriate vehicle for my goal?

> If no to either: **skip.**

### 2. Concentration (2 min)
- For ETFs/funds: is top 10 holdings less than 60%?
- Is sector exposure appropriate for my portfolio?

> If extreme concentration is unwanted: **skip.**

### 3. Cost (1 min)
- For funds: expense ratio under 0.5%?
- Liquidity adequate (tight spread, decent volume)?

> If high cost without compelling reason: **skip.**

### 4. Valuation (3 min)
- Forward P/E vs peer median: cheap, fair, or expensive?
- Vs own historical average: cheap, fair, or expensive?

> If expensive on both AND no growth justification: **skip.**

### 5. Trend (2 min)
- Above or below 200-day moving average?
- Distance from 52-week high/low?

> If buying: prefer above 200-day MA, not at extreme highs.

### 6. Sentiment extremes (3 min)
- Put/Call ratio extreme (< 0.3 or > 2.0)?
- All-timeframe Strong Buy or Strong Sell from aggregators?

> If extreme sentiment in direction you'd be trading: **caution**, possibly contrarian.

### 7. Macro fit (3 min)
- Does current environment favor this sector / asset class?
- Any obvious macro headwind brewing?

> If macro headwind without specific catalyst: **reduce position or skip.**

## Quick screen verdict

> **🟢 Passes all 7 checks:** do the full [[worksheet-template]] before sizing.
>
> **🟡 Passes 4-6 checks:** full worksheet warranted but expect mixed verdict.
>
> **🔴 Passes 3 or fewer:** skip. Don't spend more time on this symbol.

## When to use this vs full worksheet

**Use quick screen when:**
- You have many candidates to filter through (e.g., screening a watchlist)
- A friend asks "what about XYZ stock?"
- You're considering a quick tactical position
- You want to decide whether a symbol deserves deeper analysis

**Use full worksheet when:**
- You're seriously considering a new position
- The quick screen says GO
- The position would be meaningful size (>2% of portfolio)
- You need to document your reasoning for future review
